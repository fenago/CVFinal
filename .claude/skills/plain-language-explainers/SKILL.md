# Plain-language explainers

- One idea per paragraph. Short sentences. Define every term the first time.
- Use an analogy, then the real thing: "WebAssembly is like a pre-compiled engine dropped into the browser: OpenCV was written in C++, compiled once into WebAssembly, and now runs at near-native speed on your device."
- Say what it means for the user: "Your photos never leave your device."
- Avoid: "simply", "just", unexplained acronyms, version numbers in prose.
- Good error copy = what happened + why (if known) + what to do next.
