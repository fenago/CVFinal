# HIG: accessibility and typography

Sources: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility · https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography

- Contrast minimums: text up to 17 pt → **4.5:1**. 18 pt+ → **3:1**. Bold → **3:1**.
- Hit targets: iOS **44×44 pt** (28×28 pt minimum).
- Text: "give people the option to enlarge text by at least 200 percent".
- Reduce Motion: reduce "automatic and repetitive animations, including zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion."
- "Describe your app's interface and content for VoiceOver."
- "Convey information with more than color alone."
- Typography: SF Pro is the system font. iOS default size **17 pt**, minimum **11 pt**. "Minimize the number of typefaces you use." Build hierarchy with weight, size and colour.
