# HIG: materials and Liquid Glass

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials

- "Liquid Glass forms a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation elements — like tab bars and sidebars — that floats above the content layer."
- "**Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer.** … use standard materials for elements in the content layer, such as app backgrounds." (Exception: transient controls like sliders and toggles while they're active.)
- "**Use Liquid Glass effects sparingly.** … Limit these effects to the most important functional elements in your app."
- Regular variant: "blurs and adjusts the luminosity of background content to maintain legibility". Use it for text-heavy components (alerts, sidebars, popovers).
- Clear variant: "highly translucent", for components over photos/video. If the content is bright, "consider adding a dark dimming layer of 35% opacity".
- Standard materials: ultra-thin, thin, regular (default), thick. "Thicker materials … provide better contrast for text".
- Appearance adapts to Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast settings.
